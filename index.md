## **Greetings from Vermont!**
My name is [**Sam Chevalier**](https://samchevalier.github.io/redirect/). I am an assistant professor of [electrical engineering](https://www.win.tue.nl/~wscor/woeginger/P-versus-NP.htm) at the [University of Vermont](https://www.uvm.edu/cems/ebe) (my [alma mater](https://samchevalier.github.io/photos/uvm_igert.jpg)!), where I am part of the [CREATE](https://www.uvm.edu/cems/create) research center and a faculty affiliate of the [Complex Systems Institute](https://vermontcomplexsystems.org/). Within CREATE, I run the **Grid Verification** Lab. We focus on hard computational problems that emerge from areas like [power grid operations](https://www.ercot.com/content/cdr/contours/rtmLmp.html), nonconvex optimization, and machine learning verification. Until fall of 2023, I was a [Marie Curie Postdoctoral Fellow](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/calls/msca-postdoctoral-fellowships-2021) at the [Technical University of Denmark](https://www.cee.elektro.dtu.dk/) (DTU) with [Spyros Chatzivasileiadis](http://www.chatziva.com/). Prior to DTU, I received my PhD ('21) from the [mechanical engineering department at MIT](https://meche.mit.edu/). For more information, [<ins>**download my CV**</ins>](https://samchevalier.github.io/docs/Chevalier_CV.pdf).

### **Research Questions**
Across all of our research applications, numerical optimization unites everything we do. Here are some representative **questions** we tackle:
{: .lab-tagline}

- Given a convex relaxation, what is the mechanistic source of relaxation looseness? Can targeted cuts [break the relaxation barrier](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.08722)?
- Can [sequentially tightening a convex relaxation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.38435) to global optimality outperform branch-and-bound?
- For a given computation time budget, what's the optimal amount of time to spend on pre-solve activities (e.g., [optimization based bound tightening](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.04565))?
{: .arrow-list}
